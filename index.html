chapter the first

Once upon a time, in 1976

A boy was born who would one day grow into a man of considerable wit, unrivaled charm, a hairline in noble retreat, and fifty remarkable years.

Along the way he won the heart of the fairest maiden in all the land, a woman of impeccable beauty and boundless patience, who has heard him say “as you wish” more times than either of them can count. Together they raised a band of brave and spirited children, the greatest adventure of the whole tale. The band has since grown to include the newest member of the realm, his granddaughter, Allie Rose.

Now the story reaches its most important chapter so far, and it would not be a proper tale without the people who filled the other forty-nine.

So we are borrowing a castle (well, a movie theater) for one night. Come for popcorn, cheers, and a big-screen showing of the greatest fairy tale ever filmed.
